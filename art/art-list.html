How to make each picture

  1. Open firefly.adobe.com and choose Text to image.
  2. Tap Copy on a prompt below and paste it into Firefly's text box.
  3. Set the shape (aspect ratio) shown for that section, make a few versions, and download your favorite.
  4. For landmarks and icons, remove the background with the free tool at Adobe Express and save as PNG.
  5. Rename the file to the name shown (like lobby-paris-map.png) and upload it to the art folder of your bingo-game repository, or send it to Claude. Tick the box here.

Never type another game's name into the tool. When you sign up, check that Firefly's terms allow commercial use.
