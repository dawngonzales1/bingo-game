Privacy Policy

Bingo Bloom, by Cordelle · Last updated October 15, 2026

Bingo Bloom is a free-to-play social bingo game made by Cordelle ("we", "us"). This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have. We keep it short and honest.

What we collect

  • Game progress. Your level, coins, tickets, petals, amethysts, cards, power-ups, settings and round history. This is saved on your device and, when you sign in, in our cloud database so it follows you between devices.
  • Account details. If you sign in with Facebook, Google or Apple we receive your name, a profile picture and an email address or an anonymous identifier from that provider. We never see your password.
  • Player name and photo. The name and avatar you choose are shown to other players in teams, friend lists and in-round player lists.
  • Messages and feedback. Notes you send through the in-game inbox or Send Feedback, so we can reply and improve the game.
  • Purchase records. If you buy coins, tickets, amethysts or card packs, the app store processes the payment and we receive a receipt so we can deliver the items. We never see your card number.
  • Device basics. Device type, operating system, app version and crash information, used only to keep the game running well.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not show third-party advertising.
  • We do not use your contacts, location, camera or microphone.
  • We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. Bingo Bloom is intended for adults.

Who we share with

Only the services that run the game: Google Firebase (sign-in and cloud save), Apple App Store and Google Play (payments), and RevenueCat (purchase receipts). Each is bound by its own privacy terms. We share only what each service needs to do its job.

Your choices

  • Play without signing in. Your progress then stays on your device only.
  • Change your player name and photo at any time in the Style Studio.
  • Ask us to delete your account and cloud data. We will do so within 30 days.
  • Clear everything yourself with Start Over in Settings.

Fair play

Bingo Bloom is a game of chance for entertainment only. Every odd is shown in the game. There are no real-money prizes and nothing you win can be exchanged for money.
